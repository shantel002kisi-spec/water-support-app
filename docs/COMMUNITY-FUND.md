# Community Fund project: Water Support Without Barriers

The intended application is a community-led assisted support pilot, using the app to help residents understand correspondence, prepare support requests and track next steps. It is not a WRMP innovation submission or an application for a personal portfolio.

## Proposed activities to validate with residents

- Accessible local bill-reading and support drop-ins, with telephone/offline alternatives.
- Budget-confidence workshops and affordability-support signposting.
- Trained volunteers and community partners helping residents prepare for conversations; qualified agencies handle regulated debt advice.
- Anonymous consultation and feedback informing delivery and improvement.

These are proposed activities, not confirmed partners, delivered sessions or demonstrated outcomes. The applicant must supply actual local consultation, delivery commitments, a justified budget and evaluation plan.

## Fund alignment and eligibility

Official guidance: https://www.wessexwater.co.uk/in-your-community/community-fund

The fund offers up to £4,000 for up to 12 months. Eligible applicants include community groups with charitable aims, charities, CICs and parish councils based and operating in the Wessex Water region. Governance and financial rules apply; consult the correct foundation. An individual cannot establish eligibility simply by having built this app.

Applications close Monday 19 October 2026. Quartet lists 11am, while Somerset's current grants listing states 10am. Confirm the precise local deadline and use the foundation's official application route. This app exports preparation material only.

## Working project tools

Open `/?view=community`. Draft seven evidence sections, add costs and requested grant shares, identify other funding, record completed local sessions with anonymous learning, export a worksheet, and back up/restore the project independently of personal cases.

The budget flags aggregate requests above £4,000. Attendance is labelled as attendance rather than unique people. Records are local and user-entered; no verified impact, eligibility decision, grant submission or team database is provided. Personal household information belongs in separate private case records, not project exports.

## Before applying

Confirm applicant organisation, service area and foundation, governance documents, actual costs/quotes, resident involvement, access and safeguarding arrangements, confirmed delivery partners, dates after award and measurable outcomes. Submit through the official foundation application, with requested supporting documents.

The earlier water-security lab remains in source as a separate exploratory module; it is no longer the featured project or navigation route for this Community Fund app.
