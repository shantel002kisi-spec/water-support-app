# Water-security challenge alignment

Official source: [Wessex Water WRMP 2029 option suggestions](https://marketplace.wessexwater.co.uk/challenges/water-resources-management-plan-wrmp-2029-option-suggestions), checked 6 October 2026. The planning horizon is 2030–2080 and the proposal deadline is 23 October 2026. Use the current official Option Proposal Form and the submission instructions on that page; the app's exported text worksheet is not that form.

## What the system does

| Brief objective | Working feature | Limit of the claim |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Increase available supply | Models a separately specified supply option, reliability and environmental reserve | App produces no water; engineering, resource availability and permits require evidence |
| Reduce customer demand | Records cumulative meter readings and compares daily use before/after a selected change; tests participation/rebound/persistence assumptions | User-entered observations are unverified; differences do not establish causality |
| Environmental resilience | Documents safeguards, drought dependencies, protected sites and waterbody risks; runs an illustrative stress scenario | No hydrological/ecological assessment, carbon quantification or regulatory approval is included |
| Long-term planning | Interactive pathway from 2030 to 2080 with explicit rollout assumptions | Scenario arithmetic, not a population/hydrological forecast or a formal WRMP model |
| Customer service | Accessible letter OCR, support cases, drafts, contact histories, checklists and follow-up dates | Independent organiser, with no live provider or meter integration |
| Reviewable proposal | Evidence prompts, visible gaps, validated local backups and worksheet export | No novelty approval, supplier certification or automatic submission |

## Critical eligibility gap

The brief explicitly excludes technology/services supporting options already under consideration. Those activities include water efficiency, metering and several supply interventions. A generic efficiency app or a service layer attached to an existing option should not be presented as qualifying simply because it contains calculators.

The candidate concept is an inclusive, evidence-led support and appraisal platform that links correspondence, action completion, recorded contact outcomes and observed use. **This description is a hypothesis for discussion, not an established novel option.** A proposer must identify a genuinely additional intervention/resource and demonstrate the difference from current planning activities. Seek Wessex Water feedback before asserting eligibility. No specific new technology, research results or independently verified pilot evidence were supplied.

## Evidence-led pilot proposal

1. Confirm the proposed intervention is additional and outside the excluded existing options. Document comparison and Marketplace feedback.
2. Recruit a consent-based pilot with accessible and offline participation routes. Do not compromise essential water use or disadvantage customers with additional needs.
3. Define intervention/control groups, baseline/post periods, primary outcome and stop criteria before measurement. Determine an appropriate sample and duration with an evaluator.
4. Obtain reliable meter data, occupancy information and relevant weather/seasonal context. Separate leakage resolution from behavioural change and account for concurrent interventions.
5. Measure adjusted demand effects, participation, retained benefit and customer-service outcomes. Independently review the analysis; do not extrapolate a short observation directly to 2080.
6. Assess engineering, resource, environmental, consent, stakeholder and cost dependencies for any separate supply component. Establish safeguards with qualified partners.
7. Revisit the option as climate, population, regulation and participation change. Treat the app's pathway as an explicit scenario, not a forecast.

## Evidence required before submission

- Proposer identity and accountable delivery partners.
- Novel intervention/resource and documented additionality.
- Mechanism, location/resource zone, baseline and uncertainty, without double counting.
- Credible pilot data or an evidence-generation plan; no invented achieved savings.
- Delivery schedule, costs, dependencies and funding request if relevant.
- Environmental and social assessments, consent/permit position, and actual supplier/BAF evidence.
- Completed current official form, reviewed claims and supporting material.

## Calculation boundaries

Demand scenario: households × household litres/day ÷ 1,000,000 × participation × gross reduction × (1 − rebound) × retained saving × rollout. Separate net supply: max(0, supply × reliability × rollout − environmental reserve). Percentages are fractions. Stress factors (50% of modelled demand savings and 60% of dependable supply) are hypothetical sensitivities, not climate predictions.

Meter comparison uses total volume/days in each period; intervals crossing the intervention date are excluded. Readings must come from one unchanged meter and may not decrease or duplicate dates. This is descriptive arithmetic, without verification, controls, weather/occupancy adjustment or causal attribution.
